If macOS blocks Pulsar

Pulsar is not notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS may say it cannot check it for malicious software. This is expected. You only do this once.

1. Double click Pulsar.app. When the warning appears, click Done (or Cancel).
2. Open System Settings, then Privacy and Security.
3. Scroll down to Security. Next to the message about Pulsar, click Open Anyway.
4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click Open Anyway again.

From now on Pulsar opens normally.
